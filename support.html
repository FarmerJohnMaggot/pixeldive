Pixel Dive · Support

How to play

Hold to get ready, let go to jump. In the air, hold to tuck and spin, and let go to straighten out before the water. Go in straight, head or feet first: a Rip, with almost no splash, scores most.

Medals and the tour

Each stop has two events. In the Judges' Call the judges call three dives, and each one you land moves you up a medal. The Big dive is three dives whose scores add up to a medal. A medal is worth stars: bronze 1, silver 2, gold 3.

A medal in both events at a stop opens the next stop. A continent opens when you have finished the last stop of the one before and won half of its stars. Asia also opens straight away when you buy the World Tour.

The World Tour and the Tower

Europe's nine stops and the daily challenge are included. The World Tour is a single in-app purchase that adds the other five continents and the Tower. You can buy it from the map or from Settings; "Restore purchases" in Settings brings it back on a new device.

The Tower opens once you own the World Tour, have dived every stop on the tour and have won 60% of its stars. Each board on the Tower has a rival; beat them to open the next board up.

Ads

Ads are optional and only on iPhone and iPad. If you haven't bought the World Tour, you can choose to watch a short ad for three more daily tries, one retry of a missed Judges' Call, or a new swimsuit colour. World Tour owners never see ads. Where a consent choice applies, "Ad privacy choices" in Settings lets you change it.

Daily reminder

On iPhone and iPad you can turn on "Daily reminder" in Settings. The game then reminds you once a day, at about 6 pm, if you have not dived that day's challenge, for up to a week after you last opened the game. It is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off there at any time.

Controls

Touch, keyboard (Space or Enter to dive, Escape to pause) and game controllers (A to dive, B or Menu to pause) all work, in every menu too.

Your progress

Progress is saved on your device and, if iCloud is on, follows you to your other devices. See the privacy policy.
