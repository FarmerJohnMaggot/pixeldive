How to play

Hold to get ready, let go to jump. In the air, hold to tuck and spin, and let go to straighten out before the water. Flick right (or press X / R1 on a controller) to twist. Go in straight, head or feet first: a Rip, with almost no splash, scores most.

Medals and the tour

Each stop's Big dive is three dives, from 12 m, 16 m and the top; their scores add up to a medal. A medal opens the next stop, and you can skip one stop if it's giving you trouble. A continent opens when you have every medal but one on the one before. The Tower opens after Europe, and its higher boards open as you finish each continent.

Controls

Touch, keyboard (Space or Enter to dive, X or Right arrow to twist, Escape to pause) and game controllers (A to dive, X or R1 to twist, B or Menu to pause) all work, in every menu too.

Your progress

Progress is saved on your device and, if iCloud is on, follows you to your other devices. See the privacy policy.
