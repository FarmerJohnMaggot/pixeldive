Pixel Dive · Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Pixel Dive is made by Farmer John Maggot, an independent developer in the United Kingdom.

We collect no data

Pixel Dive collects no personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it works offline. Nothing about you or how you play is sent to us or to anyone else.

Progress stays on your device

Your medals, best scores, settings and diver look are saved on your device only. Deleting the app removes them.

iCloud and Game Center

If iCloud is on, Apple keeps a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account so it can follow you to your other devices. If you are signed in to Game Center, Apple handles your achievements and leaderboard scores. Both are provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We never see this data.

Purchases

Pixel Dive has no in-app purchases and no ads.

Children

No personal data is collected from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
