Pixel Dive · Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Pixel Dive is made by Farmer John Maggot, an independent developer in the United Kingdom.

What we collect

We, the developer, collect no personal data. Pixel Dive has no accounts, analytics or tracking of its own, and the game plays offline. The only third party involved is Google's ad service, described below, and only on iPhone and iPad when a player chooses to watch an ad.

Reminders

If you turn on "Daily reminder", the reminders are set on your device by the game itself. No notification is sent from a server and no device token or other data leaves your device for them.

Ads

On iPhone and iPad, players who haven't bought the World Tour can choose to watch a rewarded ad (for example, for more daily-dive tries). Ads are never shown unless you tap to watch one, and players who own the World Tour never see ads. The Mac version has no ads.

The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads and measure them, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device type, IP address, approximate location, how you interact with ads, and, only if you allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier. Google handles this data under Google's privacy policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

The first time you choose to watch an ad, iOS asks whether Pixel Dive may track you; you can say no and still get every reward, and change this later in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland, Google's consent form also asks for your choice; once you have seen that form, you can change your choice in the game's Settings under "Ad privacy choices" for as long as ads are on (the row goes when you buy the World Tour, because ads stop).

Progress stays on your device

Your medals, best scores, settings and diver look are saved on your device. Deleting the app removes them from the device.

iCloud and Game Center

If iCloud is on, Apple keeps a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account so it can follow you to your other devices. If you are signed in to Game Center, Apple handles your achievements and leaderboard scores. Both are provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We never see this data.

Purchases

Europe is free. The World Tour (the other five continents and the Tower) is a one-off in-app purchase, handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Children

Pixel Dive is made for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
